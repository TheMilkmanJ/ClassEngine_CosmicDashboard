## 1. The problem the field has

QCD allows a CP-violating angle θ̄. The neutron electric dipole moment bounds |θ̄| ≲ 10⁻¹⁰. Why so small?

The standard answer is the axion (or another dynamical relaxation). **This model does not offer a competing answer.** It offers a different kind of statement: the angle is outside its jurisdiction.

---

## 2. The constitutional silence

The scope theorem (recorded with the constitution): the medium is EM-neutral and universality-walled. It carries no account of the strong sector's phases and cannot distinguish quark flavours. θ̄ is therefore **outside the model's jurisdiction, permanently**.

Consequences:

- PRTOE neither explains nor predicts θ̄.
- It does not replace the axion mechanism, demote it, or rephrase it in medium language.
- The silence is **load-bearing and falsifiable**: if any future version of the model needed θ̄ — or needed to distinguish quark flavours — to fit data, the constitution is violated and the model dies.

What the model refuses to explain is part of its shape.

---

## 3. What this file is not

**Not unfinished.** A short abstention is the finished form. Expanding it into a "solution" of strong CP would be a constitutional change, not a polish pass.

**Not the medium's confining / g_p proposals.** Elsewhere the corpus explores whether a *dark* confining sector can source quantities such as the proton magnetic moment factor g_p. That is a different object. Those notes must not be read as a solution of QCD's θ̄, and they are not folded into this file. If they ever required flavour-distinguishing QCD phases, they would conflict with the silence stated here — and that conflict would be the model's problem, not a licence to "complete" this page with a mechanism.

**Not an arXiv paper.** An abstention with a model-internal falsifier is legitimate hygiene; it is not a result about nature that ships alone.

---

## 4. Why the file exists

Big-claim hygiene cuts both ways. A model that lists what it explains must list what it abstains from with equal prominence, or the conquest list is advertising. Strong CP is the canonical abstention — the survivor the scope theorem named.

Deep audits (2026-07-18/19) passed this page as a clean constitutional silence. The 2026-08-02 refresh only clarifies boundaries and removes shout-caps; the substance is unchanged.
