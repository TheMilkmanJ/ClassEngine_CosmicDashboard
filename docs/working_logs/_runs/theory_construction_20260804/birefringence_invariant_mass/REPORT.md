## What was chased

### Candidate statement (CANDIDATE on entry)

> **IM-B:** Free photons are massless (\(m^2=E^2-p^2=0\)). In a sufficiently condensed / collective EM configuration, the **system** can carry nonzero **invariant mass** \(M_\mathrm{inv}=\sqrt{E_\mathrm{tot}^2-p_\mathrm{tot}^2}\). That system rest energy supplies a preferred rest frame and mass-like dispersion for the collective mode, which was hoped to open a door to cosmic polarization rotation (birefringence) that vacuum-null \(m_\gamma=0\) seemed to forbid.

### Why it felt like “what the model was saying”

Hunt language often collapses several different “mass” ideas:

| “Mass” idea | Object | Parity | Opens photon rotation? |

| Vacuum Proca \(m_\gamma\) | Single photon | even (scalar mass) | No by itself; gauge/bounds issues |

| **System invariant mass** | Multi-photon / cavity / condensate system | **even** | **No** — rest mass has no handedness |
| EM-anomalous \(\theta F\tilde F\) coupling | Pseudoscalar–photon | odd×odd→even operator, odd effect | **Yes for \(\beta\)** — but needs EM charge / anomaly the medium lacks |

The residual kept saying “something mass-like about light.” **IM-B answers a different question** (does the light *system* have rest energy?) than birefringence asks (does polarization of free-streaming CMB get a parity-odd twist from the medium?).

1. Invariant mass of multi-photon systems is **standard relativity** — not exotic.

## Should-not-exist / kill track (adversarial — double scrutiny)

### Kill K1 — Wrong observable

Cosmic birefringence (isotropic \(\beta\), \(\langle EB\rangle\)) is a **parity-odd** polarization rotation.  
**Invariant mass is a Lorentz scalar** (parity-even). A scalar rest energy does not distinguish left- from right-circular polarization. **No handedness ⇒ no \(\beta\).**

### Kill K2 — L1 / census wall (corpus standing theorem)

Laws README worked example + Failures Ledger six-route closure:

- Medium’s universal coupling is **gravity** (parity-even, no handedness).  
- Handedness lives in **EM**; medium is **EM-neutral** ⇒ no \(\theta F\tilde F\) wire to photons.  
- Gravity-only transfer of EM stress-energy carries **parity-even** \(T_{\mu\nu}\) only; \(F\tilde F\) is topological and does not source the metric.  

**IM-B does not grant EM charge.** Condensing a dark-U(1) medium activates dark/gravity couplings, not an EM terminal (laws README: regime change moves mass/f_n, not charge).

### Kill K3 — Epoch window still closed

`scripts/birefringence_window.py` (log in `logs/`): at model \(z_x\sim10^5\), normal fraction at recombination \(f_n\sim10^{-8}\) (n=4). Coupling-open and free-stream windows do not overlap. **Invariant mass does not move \(z_x\) or \(f_n\).** Reopening still requires a genesis-side \(z_x\) near equality (registered two-way bet), not a mass rename.

### Kill K4 — Free-streaming CMB ≠ condensed system

After last scattering, CMB photons free-stream. Their single-particle invariant mass remains **zero**. Any collective \(M_\mathrm{inv}\) of a pre-decoupling condensed EM configuration is **not** the propagator of free CMB photons at \(z\sim1100\). Conflating system rest mass with photon Proca mass is a category error.
