## 3. Physical reasonableness (not a number)

1. **Josephson / Josephson-like grammar is native.** The AD-direct route uses a DC-Josephson form \(I_L\propto\mu\sin\Delta\theta\). A plasma frequency for a driven junction phase is the natural scale that sets the amplitude of the AC cos-drive once the partner phase winds at \(\dot\theta\).

2. **Two cos terms on one phase is already the model’s formalization.** Stage 8 writes both \(U_\mathrm{pin}\) and \(U_J\) with shared \(\chi\). A_ωJ does not invent a second phase or a new field; it asserts that the *curvature of the driven term* is fixed by seat content rather than left free for \(\eta\)-fitting.

3. **Independence is the honest reading of “micro.”** If the seat term is a real operator (tenth channel), its low-energy strength at \(T_\mathrm{sph}\) is a property of that operator and the bath, not of the observed baryon density. A_ωJ is the statement that we treat that strength as primary.

4. **No conflict with overdamping or pin hierarchy.** Overdamping \(\Gamma_\varphi/\dot\theta\gg 1\) and \(m_1\ll\dot\theta\) are independent of \(\omega_J\)’s origin. They constrain *whether the rectifier formula applies*, not whether a seat plasma frequency may exist.

5. **Naming collision does not kill the object.** Jeans \(\omega_J=\sqrt{4\pi G\rho}\) is a different quantity (gravitational). Its existence elsewhere does not forbid a junction plasma frequency; it only forbids re-using that formula under the same name as a land (`C2` **DEAD** / WRONG_OBJECT in the roster).
