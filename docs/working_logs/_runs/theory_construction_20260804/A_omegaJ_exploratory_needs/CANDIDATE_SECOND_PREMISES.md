\[

## P2-3 — **A_Jos-plasma** (Josephson circuit plasma law — *law stretch*)

### Statement (CANDIDATE)

> Stretch native AD-Josephson grammar: the seat–visible junction is dual to a driven Josephson element whose **plasma frequency** is
> \[
> \omega_J^2 \;=\; \frac{2e\,I_c}{\hbar\,C_J}
> \quad\text{(or the model’s natural-unit analogue)}
> \]
> where **critical current** \(I_c\) is fixed by seat supercurrent / cos-amplitude micro content at \(T_\mathrm{sph}\), and **junction capacitance** \(C_J\) (phase stiffness dual) is fixed by seat geometry / density of states — **neither** taken from \(\eta\), **neither** silent-\(v_L\).

This **stretches** stocked laws: corpus has Josephson *current* form for AD-direct, **not** a priced \((I_c, C_J)\) pair for the overdamped rectifier.

### can-exist

- AD-direct already uses \(I_L\propto\mu\sin\Delta\theta\) grammar; plasma frequency is the natural AC scale of that grammar.  
- Maps onto \(\omega_J^2=J_\mathrm{seat}/\chi\) with \(J_\mathrm{seat}\sim I_c\) scale and \(\chi\sim C_J\) scale — same residual dimension, new micro vocabulary.  
- Deep-frozen winding \(\dot\theta\) already supplies the AC drive; plasma is a separate object that *can* sit beside drive.

### should-not-exist (adversarial)

- **Two free constants replace one:** unless \(I_c\) and \(C_J\) are independently micro-priced, this is A_ωJ with circuit hats (Charge A).  
- Corpus has **no** \(C_J\), **no** seat \(I_c\) number, **no** map from tenth-channel UV to circuit parameters.  
- Overdamped class **cancels** stiffness — inventing \(C_J\) that never enters \(R\) except via ratio re-opens the same underdetermination.  
- High risk of laundering back-solve by choosing \(I_c/C_J\).

### What would score against band

**No circuit micro in corpus → no score.**
