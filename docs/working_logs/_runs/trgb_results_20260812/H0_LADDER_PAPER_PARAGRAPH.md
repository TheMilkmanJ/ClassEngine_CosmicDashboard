# H0 ladder paragraph (paper-ready draft)

**Stack:** DESI-DR2 BAO + Planck (lowℓ + plik-lite + lensing) + ACT DR2 + SPT-3G lite + Pantheon+, with either SH0ES or TRGB as the local H0 anchor. Matched dyad vs ΛCDM+m_ν twins; GetDist, 30% burn-in. **Not** nested evidence.

---

Under the DESI-DR2 multi-survey stack with a **SH0ES** distance-ladder prior, the dyad model prefers a higher expansion rate than its ΛCDM+m_ν twin:  
**H₀ = 70.30 ± 0.54 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹** (dyad) versus **68.73 ± 0.25** (ΛCDM), a difference of **ΔH₀ ≈ +1.6 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹**, with a modestly better mean χ² for the dyad.  
Replacing SH0ES by a **TRGB** H0 anchor on the same CMB+BAO+SN(+ACT/SPT) likelihood **removes that pull**: both models return **Planck-like** values,  
**H₀ = 68.90 ± 0.60** (dyad) and **68.39 ± 0.26** (ΛCDM), **ΔH₀ ≈ +0.5 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹**, with essentially tied χ².  
Thus the dyad’s high-H₀ preference on this stack is **ladder-conditional**, not a stack-wide requirement of the CMB+BAO data alone. Nested Bayes factors for these twins remain pending production evidence runs.
