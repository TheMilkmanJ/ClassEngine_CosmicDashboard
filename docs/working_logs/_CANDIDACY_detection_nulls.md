### Why extraction fails

1. **Forced only given the constitution.** Every null is a corollary of model structure
   (condensate quanta, EM neutrality, conserved dark charge, portal scales). Without those
   premises the ceilings are unmotivated. Neutrino-mbb works because the *input* is stated as a
   free hypothesis and the *output* is non-trivial external arithmetic (NuFIT → m_ββ window).
   Here the inputs *are* the model, and the outputs are dimensional silence.

2. **Hypothesis→consequence does not yield a paper.** Reframing as “if m ≃ 2×10⁻²⁰ eV and
   only gravitational-strength matter coupling, then σ is absurd” is true and checkable as
   arithmetic — and is one paragraph of dimensional analysis, not a short paper. “If a dark
   charge is exactly conserved, tree-level annihilation vanishes” is a sentence. Neither is
   novel relative to the ultra-light / fuzzy / wave-DM literature, which already knows WIMP
   and annihilation channels do not apply.

3. **No independent external engagement.** Supertrace-note compares two published conditions.
   Lattice-tc-gap names a missing lattice number. Radio-lattice gives free ε + atomic weights.
   Detection nulls do not resolve a literature gap, map a hypothesis onto external data
   products, or produce a forecast an instrument can target. Continued silence earns the model
   nothing (file’s own Tsirelson-class posture); only a *positive* WIMP/annihilation claim would
   kill — that is internal exposure bookkeeping, not a public claim about nature.

4. **No reproducible computation object.** The quoted σ and σv ceilings are light-compute
   order-of-magnitude statements with no dedicated script, control table, or literature
   cross-check trail. A referee “checking” them would re-derive G²m²-class scaling in minutes.
   That is correct physics and still not manuscript content.

5. **Shape is multi-channel exposure, not one claim.** The two files are siblings by design
   (underground silence → sky silence). Bundling them into one “purchased silences” note still
   yields a model scorecard plus kill conditions, not a single checkable result. Redirects
   (radio sub-lattice, CMB-S4 Majoron corner, helicity, ε-dipole) point at *other* papers
   already packaged or not yet ready.

6. **Falsification asymmetry is model-internal.** “One confirmed halo-attributable recoil or
   annihilation line kills the model” is real and unhedgeable *for the framework*. It is not a
   result a journal publishes without the framework paper it protects.
