## 1. The model's structure: the neutrino mass is medium-sourced

In this model the neutrino mass is not generated by an ordinary Higgs Yukawa term. It is
medium-sourced, through the inverse-seesaw/Majoron construction, with the lepton-number-breaking
scale tied to the condensate and the recorded relation between the dark-energy scale and the
lightest neutrino mass. The structural claim is that the neutrino mass sits in the medium sector,
not the electroweak Higgs sector.
