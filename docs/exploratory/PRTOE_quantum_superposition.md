"The particle is in two places at once, until looked at." Neither clause survives inspection:

not a particle smeared into ghosts; it is **one definite configuration of the medium** whose
decomposition into our particle-basis has two terms. The strangeness is inherited from asking
medium-questions in particle-language. (Water does not paradox itself by carrying two

interference = the medium adding its own configurations; the Born rule's arena = the mode

an irreversible recording, and that irreversibility is why it has a direction.**
